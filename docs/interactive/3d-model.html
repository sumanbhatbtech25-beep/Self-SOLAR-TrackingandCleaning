      Build notes

      1. Run the axle through the panel's centre of mass. A small servo then only fights friction and wind, not the panel's weight.
      2. Power both servos from the 5 V buck output, not the ESP32's 3.3 V pin. Add a 470 µF capacitor across the servo rail and share ground with the ESP32.
      3. Keep the LDRs on GPIO 34/35. Those are ADC1 pins; ADC2 stops working once Wi-Fi is on.
      4. Wire the INA219 high-side: panel + to VIN+, VIN− to the load. Add the fuse before it.
      5. Tilt travel is limited to ±60°. Beyond that the panel waits at the limit, which the diagnostics report as normal.
      6. The arc wiper clears the centre of the panel; the far corners need a longer blade or a linear rail later.
