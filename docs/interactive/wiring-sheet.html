Solar Rig Wiring

Complete connections for the single-ESP32 tracker with battery storage: the panel charges a 12 V battery through the INA219 and a charge controller, and a buck converter turns the battery into 5 V for the ESP32 and servos. Wire it in the order of the list, ticking each one off.

Schematic

5 V 3.3 V Ground Signal I²C Panel current path Battery 12 V Optional, fit later

Labels like 5V, 3V3 and the ground symbol mean "connect to that rail". Every ground symbol is the same common ground, which is the battery −. BAT+ is the battery positive after fuse F2. Panel − goes only to the charge controller, never to ground. Pin order shown is the common DOIT ESP32 DevKit V1 (30-pin); check your board's printed labels, as some clones swap positions.

Bring-up order

  1. Battery and buck. Wires 1–7, ESP32 not yet connected. Turn the buck's screw until the 5 V rail reads 5.0–5.2 V.
  2. ESP32. Wires 8, 9 and 11. Upload a blink sketch; the 3V3 rail should read 3.2–3.4 V.
  3. INA219. Wires 12–15. Run tests/i2c_scan; it should find 0x40.
  4. Battery sense. Wires 20–22. Run tests/battery_check and compare with a multimeter.
  5. DS18B20. Wires 29–32. Warm the probe in your hand and watch it rise.
  6. LDRs. Wires 23–28. Covering one LDR should drop its reading sharply.
  7. Capacitor, then servos. Wire 10 first, then 33–35 and 36–38.
  8. Panel last. Wires 16–19 in sunlight. The controller's charging light should come on and the INA219 should show current.

Watch out for

  • Battery first, panel second. Connect the battery to the controller before the panel, and disconnect the panel first. The controller detects 12 V from the battery.
  • Panel − to the controller only (wire 19). Many cheap controllers switch the negative line. Tying panel − to ground bypasses them and overcharges the battery.
  • Fuse F2 near the battery. A shorted 7 Ah battery can push over 100 A. Keep F2 within 15 cm of the + terminal.
  • Servo power. Servo red wires go to the 5 V rail, never the ESP32's 3V3 pin. Without C1, servo moves can reset the ESP32.
  • USB plus battery. When uploading over USB, unplug wire 8 (VIN) unless your board has a diode on its USB 5 V line.
  • Moving cables. LDR, DS18B20 and wiper leads ride on the tilting panel. Leave a slack loop at the axle for ±60° travel.
